# Model intent

Linear-elastic spatial beam reconstruction of the above-ground steel support; retained P2-P6 members, equivalent P1/panel gravity/snow/wind contact forces. Main purpose is numerical reproduction of stated SAP results under user scope.

No panels, proprietary rails, soil/embedment, bolt contact, local plate buckling or seismic action. Section stresses are beam-theory values, not bolt-hole hot spots. Validation requires comparison of all three stated targets and physical/load-path verification. Confidence in nominal dimensions is high; purlin layout, releases, weights, wind interpretation and stress-case equivalence are uncertain.
